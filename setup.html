🎯 Welcome to Automation Selector Picker

Your web automation element picker is ready. Just one quick setup step!

1 Assign Your Keyboard Shortcut

Chrome requires you to manually assign keyboard shortcuts for extensions to avoid conflicts with your existing shortcuts.

Click the button below to open Chrome's keyboard shortcuts manager:

When prompted, press: Cmd+Shift+L (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+L (Windows/Linux)

2 Test Your Shortcut

Once assigned, visit any website and press your keyboard shortcut to activate the picker overlay.

You should see a crosshair cursor appear, ready to select elements.

💡 Alternative: Use the Quick Button

If you prefer not to set up a keyboard shortcut, you can always click the extension icon and select "Open picker now" to activate the picker.
